1. Introduction

1.1 Purpose & scope

This guide explains how to use the Payroll System, the payroll and human-resources application of Sister of Mary of Banneux, Inc. It walks through every module—from maintaining employee records and uploading biometric attendance, to processing daily time records (DTR), generating payroll periods, computing payroll, and printing payslips and DTR reports.

The guide is written for two audiences: new users, who should read the chapters in order, and experienced users, who can use the table of contents as a quick reference.

1.2 System overview

The application is a secure, web-based system that centralizes employee master data, attendance (DTR), payroll computation, loans, and reporting. Access is role-based: each screen and action is guarded by permissions (see Chapter 16 and Appendix A), so users only see the menu items they are allowed to use.

Employees
Master records — names, positions, groups, areas, rates
Attendance
Biometric files, shift codes, schedules, DTR
Payroll
Periods, cutoff dates, compute & release
Reports
Payslips and DTR reports, printable

1.3 Key features

  • Employee management — add, search, group, and maintain employee records with employment and tenured statuses.
  • Biometric uploading — upload raw biometric batches and re-process them to build attendance records.
  • Timekeeping — shift codes, employee shift schedules (templates and per-date), and cutoff dates.
  • DTR processing — process attendance for all employees at once or per employee, with a reviewable daily-time-record view.
  • Payroll periods — generate a full year of periods from a cutoff scheme, then walk each period through open → processing → closed → released.
  • Payroll computation — lock DTR, compute payroll, review employee summaries, and release payslips.
  • Reports — print DTR per employee or for all employees; print payslips per employee.
  • Loans — record loans with a deduction frequency (every payday, every 15th, or end of month).
  • Maintenance — loan types, groups, positions, areas of assignment, and holidays.
  • Administration — users, roles, and a permission/access-control matrix.
  • Security — login with rate limiting, two-factor authentication (2FA), and passkeys.

2. Getting Started

2.1 Opening the application

Open a modern web browser (Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Safari) and go to the address provided by your administrator — for example http://127.0.0.1:8000 on the office network. The application adapts to desktop, tablet, and phone screens, but a desktop screen is recommended for payroll processing.

Tip
Bookmark the main address and keep the tab open for the fastest day-to-day use. The session is also remembered on the computer you trust.

2.2 Logging in

  1. On the Sign in screen, enter your username in the first field.
  2. Enter your password in the second field.
  3. Optionally tick Remember me so the browser keeps you signed in on that device.
  4. Click Sign in.
Important
After a number of failed attempts, the system temporarily locks the sign-in form and shows a countdown before you can try again. This protects your account from password guessing. Wait for the countdown to finish rather than refreshing repeatedly.

2.3 Forgotten password

  1. On the sign-in screen, click the forgot password link.
  2. Enter your account's email address.
  3. Open the password-reset email you receive, follow the link, and choose a new password.
  4. Return to the sign-in screen and sign in with the new password.

2.4 Account security

From Settings → Security (Chapter 17) you can strengthen your account with two-factor authentication (2FA) — signing in then requires a one-time code from an authenticator app — and passkeys, which let you sign in with your device's built-in biometrics (fingerprint, face, or PIN) instead of a password.

Note
Your administrator may require certain security measures (such as 2FA) before you can access payroll data. Keep your recovery codes in a safe place when you enable 2FA.

3. The Dashboard

The Dashboard is the home screen you land on after signing in. It gives an at-a-glance summary of the whole payroll operation.

3.1 Statistic cards

Four summary cards at the top of the screen show live totals:

CardWhat it shows
LoansTotal number of loan records on file.
EmployeesTotal number of employee records.
UsersTotal number of system user accounts.
PeriodsTotal number of payroll periods available (usually one per month of the current year).

3.2 Payroll overview chart

A bar chart displays the payroll totals per month, so you can quickly compare one period against another (for example, spotting a heavier pay period or an unusually empty one).

3.3 Recent activity feed

The activity feed logs notable events across the system — records created, updated, and deleted — together with who performed them and when. This is your first stop when checking what changed in the system.

Tip
Use the Recent Activity feed to confirm that a payroll period was processed, a payslip was released, or an employee record was modified before following up with other users.

The sidebar can be collapsed to icons with the toggle button at its top-left corner to give the workspace more room. Your name and a small menu (Profile, Security, Appearance, Sign out) appear at the bottom of the sidebar.

4.2 Page header

Each main screen has a header with a title and a short description explaining the screen's purpose, and usually an Add / New button to create records.

4.3 Data tables

Most list screens (Employees, Payroll Periods, Loans, and so on) use a standard data table with the same controls:

  • Search box — type to filter rows by name, code, or keyword.
  • Filters — screen-specific dropdowns (for example year, month, status on Payroll Periods; group on Employees).
  • Rows per page — choose how many rows are shown (for example 10, 25, 50).
  • Pagination — move through pages of results when there are more rows than fit on one page.
  • Row actions — icons or buttons at the end of each row for actions such as View, Edit, Process, or Delete.

4.4 Forms & dialogs

Creating and editing is done in a dialog window that opens on top of the list. Fill in the required fields and click Save (or Generate for batch actions). Validation messages appear under fields that need correction. Use Cancel or press Esc to close without saving.

4.5 Flash messages

After successful actions a green confirmation message appears; after errors, a red message explains what went wrong. They disappear automatically after a few seconds. If a server error message appears, take note of what you were doing and contact the administrator.

5. Employees

The Employee screen (MAIN → Employee) is where the master data for every employee is kept. All other modules refer back to these records, so keep them complete and up to date.

5.1 Viewing employees

  • Open Employee from the sidebar to see the employee table.
  • Use the search box to find an employee by name.
  • Use the group filter to narrow the list to one group (department/unit).
  • Click a row's view action to open the employee's full record.

5.2 Adding an employee

  1. Click Add employee (or New) on the Employee screen.
  2. Fill in the employee's details, including:
    • Name — first and last name exactly as used on official records.
    • Employment status — Probationary or Regular.
    • Tenured status — Tenured or Non-Tenured.
    • Position — select from the positions maintained under Maintenance, or add a new one directly from the field if available.
    • Group and Area of assignment — select from the values maintained under Maintenance.
    • Rate / pay details — the amounts used when payroll is computed.
    • Profile photo — optional image upload.
  3. Click Save. The new employee appears in the table immediately.

5.3 Editing and deleting

  • To correct details, open the employee and use the edit action, then save your changes.
  • To remove a record, use the delete action and confirm. Deletion is recorded in the Recent Activity feed.
Reminders
Check the spelling of names and the accuracy of positions, groups, and rates — these flow directly into DTR processing, payroll, and payslips. If an employee is leaving, consider what the organization's process is before deleting their historical payroll records.

6. Loans

The Loan screen (MAIN → Loan) records loans granted to employees. Loan deductions are then applied automatically when payroll is computed.

6.1 Adding a loan

  1. Open Loan from the sidebar and click Add loan.
  2. Select the employee who received the loan.
  3. Choose the loan type (maintained under Maintenance → Loan types).
  4. Enter the loan amount and any relevant dates.
  5. Set the deduction frequency:
    • Every Payday — a deduction is taken from each pay run.
    • Every 15th — deducted on the 15th of the month.
    • End of Month — deducted at the end of the month.
  6. Click Save.

6.2 Managing loans

  • Use the search box to find loans by employee or loan type.
  • Use the row edit action to change amounts, frequency, or dates.
  • Use the row delete action to remove a loan record (for example, if it was entered by mistake).

7. Biometric Uploading

The Biometric Uploading screen (MAIN → Biometric Uploading) is where raw attendance files from the biometric clocking devices are brought into the system. Each uploaded file becomes an import batch that is processed into daily time records.

7.1 Uploading a biometric file

  1. Open Biometric Uploading from the sidebar.
  2. Click the upload / new batch button and choose the biometric file from your computer.
  3. Confirm the import. The system creates a batch and begins processing the punches it contains.
  4. Check the import result message for the number of records read and any warnings.

7.2 Re-processing a batch

If a batch produced missing or incorrect records (for example, because employee data was fixed after the first import), you can re-process it: find the batch in the list and click its reprocess action. The system reads the original file again and rebuilds the attendance records with the current employee data.

7.3 Deleting a batch

To discard an incorrectly uploaded file, use the batch's delete action. This removes the batch and the records it produced.

Good practice
Upload biometric files as they become available (for example, daily or weekly). Waiting until the end of a payroll period makes problems harder to trace. After uploading, go to Process DTR (Chapter 11) to verify the attendance data per employee.

8. Shift Codes

The Shift Code screen (MAIN → Shift Code) defines the shifts that exist in the organization — for example morning, afternoon, or night shifts.

8.1 Viewing and filtering

The shift list shows every shift code together with its description. Use the search box to find a code quickly.

8.2 Adding a shift code

  1. Click Add shift code.
  2. Enter a short, unique code and a clear description (for example, AM — Morning Shift).
  3. Define the start and end times for the shift.
  4. Click Save.

8.3 Editing and deleting

  • Use the row edit action to change any detail of a shift.
  • Use the row delete action only if the shift is no longer used by any employee schedule — otherwise schedules that reference it may break.
Note
Shift codes are the building blocks used by the Employee Shift Schedule (Chapter 9). Keep codes short and consistent, and avoid creating duplicate codes with different spellings.

9. Employee Shift Schedule

The Employee Shift Schedule screen (MAIN → Employee Shift Schedule) assigns shifts to employees, either as a reusable template applied to an employee or as a per-date override for a specific day.

9.1 Scheduling approaches

ApproachWhen to use it
TemplateThe employee's usual weekly rhythm repeats every week. One template covers it.
Per-dateA specific day differs from the routine — a training day, a holiday coverage, a swap. Add just that date.

9.2 Adding a schedule

  1. Open Employee Shift Schedule and click Add schedule.
  2. Select the employee and the shift code to assign.
  3. For templates, set the days of the week the shift applies to; for per-date schedules, pick the specific date.
  4. Click Save.

9.3 Removing a schedule

Select the schedule row and use the delete action. Removing a per-date schedule restores the employee's normal template; removing a template leaves the employee without a default schedule, so add the replacement first.

10. Cutoff Dates

The Cutoff Dates screen (MAIN → Cutoff Dates) defines the cutoff scheme — the day of the month on which attendance is cut and payroll periods close. This scheme is used when payroll periods are generated (Chapter 12).

10.1 Viewing cutoff dates

The screen lists the configured cutoff dates with their labels and the day-of-month values. For example, a scheme might cut attendance on the 15th and at end of month (30th), or use a single monthly cutoff.

10.2 Adding a cutoff date

  1. Click Add cutoff date.
  2. Give the cutoff a label (for example, "Mid-month") and the day of the month it falls on.
  3. Click Save.

10.3 Editing and deleting

  • Use the row edit action to correct a cutoff's label or day.
  • Use the row delete action with care: payroll periods already generated from a cutoff are not automatically changed.
Sequence matters
Define the cutoff scheme before generating payroll periods. If the organization changes its pay schedule, update the cutoff dates first, then generate new periods (or regenerate for the affected year).

11. Process DTR (Daily Time Record)

The Process DTR screen (MAIN → Process DTR) turns raw attendance (from biometric uploads and schedules) into verified daily time records for a payroll period. This is where you confirm that each employee's time is correct before payroll is computed.

11.1 Choosing what to process

  1. Select the period (month) you are processing from the Periods field.
  2. Choose the process mode:
    • All — process every employee in the period in one run.
    • Per employee — process employees one at a time, useful when only a few employees need attention.

11.2 Processing

  • In All mode, click Process All. The system builds DTR for every employee in the period.
  • In Per employee mode, use each row's process action, or open an employee's record with the view action to inspect the Daily Time Record dialog showing that employee's dates and times.

11.3 Reviewing DTR

Rows that have not been processed yet show a pending state; processed rows show their status. Open any employee's DTR to check clock-in/clock-out times against the expected schedule before moving on to payroll.

Before you continue
When every DTR row looks correct, the period is ready to be locked and computed — continue to Chapter 13: Processing a Payroll Period.

12. Payroll Periods

The Payroll Periods screen (MAIN → Payroll Periods) is the control center for the pay calendar. It lists every payroll period with its dates and processing status, and is where you generate periods and open them for processing.

12.1 Generating yearly periods

Rather than creating one month at a time, the system can generate a period for every month of a year in a single action:

  1. Click Generate Payroll Period.
  2. Choose the year to generate (available years are shown in the dropdown).
  3. Choose the cutoff date scheme — the cutoff configuration from Chapter 10 that defines the period's cut and close dates.
  4. Click Generate.

The dialog explains: "This will generate payroll periods for the entire year based on the selected cutoff date." All twelve periods are created and appear in the list with status Open.

12.2 Period statuses

Each period carries a status that reflects where it is in the payroll lifecycle:

StatusMeaning
OpenAttendance is still being collected and verified (DTR not yet locked).
ProcessingDTR has been locked and payroll is being computed.
ClosedPayroll has been computed and is being reviewed before release.
ReleasedPayslips have been finalized and released; the period is complete.

Full details of each state and what you can do in it are in Chapter 18.

12.3 Filtering the list

  • Year — show periods of a specific year.
  • Month — jump straight to one month.
  • Status — show only Open, Processing, Closed, or Released periods.
  • Search — free-text search across the period list.

12.4 Opening a period for processing

Click a period row (or its processing action) to open the Process Period screen described in the next chapter. From there you lock DTR, compute payroll, and release payslips.

13.1 Workflow at a glance

2 — Lock & computeClick Lock DTR, then Compute PayrollMoves to Processing, then Closed
3 — Review & releaseReview summary, click Release PayslipsMoves to Released

13.2 The employee summary panel

The screen shows a summary of the period so you can see progress at a glance:

CounterMeaning
EmployeesTotal number of employees in the period.
ProcessedEmployees whose DTR has been processed.
FlaggedEmployees whose records need attention before computing.
PendingEmployees still waiting to be processed.

13.3 Step 1 — DTR collection (period is Open)

  1. Use Process DTR to build every employee's daily time records.
  2. Return to this period's Process screen and confirm the summary looks complete (no unexpected Pending or Flagged rows).
  3. When ready, click Lock DTR. Locking stops attendance changes for the period — from this point the DTR is the record of truth for the payroll run.

13.4 Step 2 — Lock & compute (period is Processing)

  1. After the lock succeeds, click Compute Payroll.
  2. The system calculates gross pay, deductions, and loan deductions for every employee using the locked DTR, rates, and loan records.
  3. Wait for the computation to finish and check for any flagged employees before proceeding.

13.5 Step 3 — Review & release (period is Closed)

  1. Review the computed results for the period.
  2. Click Release Payslips to finalize the payslips so they can be generated and printed (Chapter 14).
  3. The period becomes Released.

13.6 Re-computing a period

If corrections are needed after computing — for example a rate was wrong, or a loan was missed — the system provides a recompute action. Note that periods already released should not be recomputed without following the organization's correction procedure, since payslips may already have been printed or used for disbursement.

Locking is final
Once a period is locked, DTR for that period can no longer be changed freely. Always confirm attendance is complete and correct before clicking Lock DTR.

14. Reports

The REPORTS menu contains two reporting tools: Generate Payslip and Generate DTR. Both prepare printable documents from the payroll data — use them to produce payslips for employees and to verify attendance on paper.

14.1 Generate Payslip

  1. Open Reports → Generate Payslip.
  2. Choose the period (month) to report on.
  3. Select per employee to produce one employee's payslip, or pick from the list to view others.
  4. Review the payslip preview, then use your browser's Print command (or the screen's print button) to save or print it.
Tip
Payslips display cleanly on paper when you use the print option; choose "Save as PDF" in the print dialog if you need an electronic copy to email.

14.2 Generate DTR

  1. Open Reports → Generate DTR.
  2. Choose the period (month) to report on.
  3. Generate the DTR for all employees (a full attendance register) or per employee (one employee's daily time records).
  4. Review and print the report, or save it as a PDF from the print dialog.
Note
Reports reflect the data as it exists at the moment you generate them. For a Released period this is final; for an Open period, re-generate after any DTR changes to get the current picture.

15. Maintenance

The Maintenance screen (SETTINGS → Maintenance) holds the reference data used throughout the system. There are five maintenance modules, each a simple list that you add to, edit, and remove as needed:

ModuleUsed by
Loan typesThe Loan screen (Chapter 6) — categories such as salary advance, emergency, or equipment loan.
GroupsEmployees — department/unit the employee belongs to; also used to filter employee lists.
PositionsEmployees — the job title or role an employee holds.
Areas of assignmentEmployees — where the employee is assigned (a building, site, or station).
HolidaysThe pay calendar — official holidays relevant to attendance and payroll.

15.1 Adding a value

  1. Open Maintenance and select the module tab (Loan types, Groups, Positions, Areas of assignment, Holidays).
  2. Click Add and enter the name (and any extra fields, such as a holiday date).
  3. Click Save.

15.2 Quick-add from forms

When filling in an employee or loan form, some fields (for example Group, Position, or Area of assignment) offer an option to add a new value on the spot instead of leaving the form. Use this for genuinely new values; for typos, fix the value in Maintenance.

15.3 Editing and deleting

  • Use the row edit action to correct a label or date.
  • Use the row delete action only when the value is no longer referenced by employees, loans, or periods.
Keep it consistent
Misspelled or duplicate reference values ("Nurse", "Nurse ", "NURSE") create splits in reports and filters. Review the Maintenance lists periodically and remove or merge duplicates.

16. Users, Roles & Access Control

These three screens (SETTINGS → Users, Roles, Access Control) manage who can use the system and what each person can do.

16.1 Users

The Users screen lists every system account (name, username, status). To add a user:

  1. Click Add user.
  2. Enter the user's name and login username.
  3. A default password will be set — ask your administrator for the details.
  4. Assign the user one or more roles that grant the permissions the person needs.
  5. Click Save.

Use the row edit action to change a user's details, and the row delete action to remove an account. Users should be removed or disabled as soon as they leave the organization.

16.2 Roles

A role is a named collection of permissions — for example "Payroll Officer" or "HR Staff". Instead of assigning permissions to each user one by one, you:

  1. Click Add role on the Roles screen.
  2. Name the role.
  3. Assign the role the permissions it needs (Chapter 16.3).
  4. Attach users to the role.

When a permission needs to change, update the role once and every user attached to it inherits the change.

16.3 Access Control (permissions)

The Access Control screen shows the full permission matrix. Open a user or a role to see every module and action side by side with a toggle for each permission:

Example permissionEffect
employee-viewCan open the Employee screen and view records.
employee-create / employee-update / employee-deleteCan add, edit, or delete employee records.
payroll-periods-viewCan see the Payroll Periods screen.
archives-viewCan view archived data.

Changes take effect immediately. The sidebar and page actions adjust automatically: screens the user cannot access disappear from the menu, and actions without permission are hidden or disabled.

Principle of least privilege
Give users only the permissions their job requires. In particular, payroll computation and release permissions should be limited to the staff responsible for the pay run.

17. Settings

Your personal account settings live under Settings in the sidebar menu, or under your name in the sidebar bottom menu, with three tabs: Profile, Security, and Appearance.

17.1 Profile

View and update your own name and email address. These should always be current, because password resets and notifications are sent to the email on file.

17.2 Security

  • Password — change your password. For security, you may be asked to confirm your current password first.

17.3 Appearance

Choose the interface theme:

  • Light — bright interface.
  • Dark — dark interface, easier on the eyes in low light.
  • System — follows your device's light/dark setting automatically.

19. Frequently Asked Questions & Troubleshooting

QuestionAnswer
I can't sign in — the form shows a countdown.Too many failed attempts temporarily locked the form. Wait out the countdown, verify the username is spelled correctly, then try again.
A menu item I need is missing.Menu items are hidden by permissions. Ask the administrator to grant the corresponding permission through Access Control (Chapter 16).
I processed DTR but an employee has no records.Check that the employee's biometric data was uploaded for that period, that their shift schedule exists, and that the correct period is selected. Re-process the employee after fixing the data.
I released a payslip and then found an error.Do not silently recompute. Follow the organization's correction procedure: notify the responsible staff, recompute the period, and re-issue the affected payslip.
Can I change DTR after locking?No — locking is designed to prevent changes. Correct the underlying data (or recompute if permitted) and follow your office procedure for adjustments.
Where do payslips and DTR reports go?They are generated on screen in the Reports menu and printed, or saved as PDF from the print dialog. The system does not email them automatically.
I forgot my password.Use the forgot-password link on the sign-in screen (Section 2.3). If you cannot access the linked email, contact the administrator.
I get a "server error" message.Note what you were doing, when, and any error text, then contact the administrator with those details.
Is it safe to leave the application open?The session expires after inactivity. Still, sign out when you leave your desk and never share your password or 2FA codes.
